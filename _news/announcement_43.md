Fall 2026 at the **University of Nicosia** starts with eleven course sections, including,
for the first time, the online BBA courses _International Business_, _Mathematics for
Economics and Business_ and _Strategy and Business Policy_. Since Spring 2025 the
teaching there comes to 36 course sections of 12 courses, with 625 student registrations,
and 12 MBA theses (three completed, nine in progress). The [CV](/cv/) now separates university and college teaching and adds a
thesis-supervision section.
